    How nudges and escalations work

    Nobody has to remember. If a step slips, the right person hears about it, once per level.

    Start date moves

    Open steps re-date automatically. Agreement, laptop order and welcome reopen to be rechecked.

    New country

    No agreement template on file? A human checks local rules before anything is sent.

    Short notice

    Steps already in the past are pulled to today, not flooded as overdue.

    Silent failure

    No "bot ran ✅" by 10am means a human checks. Every send is logged. The Tasks tab works as a manual checklist.

    Rules we keep

    🤝 Human moments stay human

    The welcome, buddy intro, check-ins and exit call are nudged to a person. The bot never sends them.

    🔒 Confidential by default

    Pay, health and legal steps go by DM. /people replies are visible only to the asker. Escalations hide the detail.

    🧭 Humans decide

    "My last day is Friday" starts a pending request. People Ops confirms dates before any access changes.

    🌍 Fair across time zones

    Everything is written and async. Nudges arrive in the morning; nothing needs a live call to move.

    What we measure

    Time to full access

    Day 0 accounts ready, measured per joiner.

    Steps on time

    % of lifecycle steps done by their due date, by owner.

    Access removed in 24h

    % of leavers with all access removed within a day of their last day.

    Answered without a human

    % of /people questions matched to a current guide. Gaps become new guides.

    Guide freshness

    % of guides reviewed in the last 6 months.
