## Leave policy
Owner: People Ops · Last reviewed: Sep 2025
- Request leave in the leave tool at least 2 weeks ahead (sick leave: same day, tell your lead).
- Contractors: 20 paid days off a year, any country. Employees: local legal minimum or 20 days, whichever is higher.
- Brazil (employees): 30 days a year, can be split into up to 3 blocks, one of at least 14 days.
- Spain (contractors): same 20 days; Spanish public holidays are extra.
- Your lead approves in the leave tool. People Ops only steps in for long or unusual leave.
